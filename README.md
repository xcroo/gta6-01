DUSTHAVEN — a 3D open-world crime game in the browser. You are Jet Calloway, a newcomer driver in Dusthaven, a sun-baked desert-edge metropolis at golden hour. Run five story jobs for the fixer Delia Voss, outrun the sheriff's department across a 0–5 star wanted level, dodge the Rust Vultures, steal and drive anything with wheels, and chase side work: timed deliveries, a stunt ramp and ten sun coins hidden around the city.

The city is fully explorable on foot and by car: a street grid with downtown towers, a canal waterfront, highways, traffic AI, pedestrians who flee when things get loud, police who chase, ram and arrest, melee and pistol combat, a live circular minimap, a start screen, localStorage saves and procedural WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, shots and the K-DUST 101.3 desert-blues radio loop). Built with vendored three.js and plain static files — no build step, no network at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to drive and walk, E to enter or leave a car, mouse click to shoot, F to punch, Space for the handbrake, Esc to pause.

Screenshots in `screenshots/` were taken in headless Chrome at 1280×800 straight from the running game.
